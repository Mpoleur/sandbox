App logo, round 2: chat bubble and question mark

Four variations after Matthieu's feedback (more character, a question mark, a chat bubble). Draft, not validated. "Travel Card" is only the working name. Round 1 is unchanged in logo-concepts.html.

How to read the sheet. Same layout as round 1: the Android adaptive icon (108 dp layers, 72 dp visible, 66 dp safe circle) in circle and squircle masks, once with the guides, the monochrome layer, light and dark home screens, themed icons, and 48 dp and 32 dp. The question mark is a drawn shape, never text, and the icons contain no letters or words. Names are unchecked: I did not check any against trademarks, app stores or domains, so none may be assumed free, and all still need those checks. Bubbles are original shapes; none imitates an existing messaging, help or translation app. No red cross, flag, or religious or political symbol.

All four together, at 96, 48 and 32 dp

Variation 1: layered bubbles with a yellow tail and an orange dot

A white bubble sits on a second, tilted light-blue bubble for depth; its tail is a yellow accent and the question mark ends in an orange dot.

Works because the tilted second bubble and the coloured tail give it a crafted, layered look that plain help icons lack, and the orange dot stays a clear point at 48 dp.

Variation 2: a dialogue, question answered by an arrow

Two overlapping bubbles on dark slate: a yellow one asks with a question mark, a white one answers with a blue arrow showing the way.

Works because it tells the whole exchange (ask, then be pointed the right way) without a word, and yellow, white and slate separate clearly even at 32 dp.

Variation 3: the bubble is an info card held out

A white card with a speech tail, an orange header with three punched dots like an information panel, and a big question mark, fanned over a blue second card on taxi yellow.

Works because it merges the app's two ideas, a card you show and a question you ask, and the fanned cards give depth with flat shapes only.

Variation 4: a round balloon, the question mark is a road ending in a pin

A round white balloon holds a question mark drawn as a blue road with a dashed centre line, whose dot is an orange map pin, on green.

Works because the question becomes a route, which is the traveller's real question, "where do I go?", and the round balloon looks unlike any rectangular messaging icon.
