App logo: three concepts, with name ideas

Draft for Matthieu to choose from, not validated. "Travel Card" is only the working name. The icons contain no words or letters; names appear only beside them.

How to read the sheet. Each concept shows the Android adaptive icon (108 dp layers, 72 dp visible, 66 dp safe circle) large, in a circle and a squircle mask, once with the safe-zone guides (dashed circle = safe zone, dashed square = visible area), then on light and dark home screens at launcher size, then the monochrome version Android 13 and later uses for themed icons, then at 48 dp and 32 dp. SVG files for every concept are in this folder (icon, foreground, background, monochrome). Names: I checked none of them against trademarks, app stores or domains, so none may be assumed free. Trademark, store and domain checks are still needed before Matthieu picks. No red cross, no flags, no religious or political symbol is used.

Concept 1: a wordless speech bubble

Idea: a card that speaks without words, a speech bubble in the calm blue of the app with a green tick for "understood", which is exactly what the app delivers.

Works because a bubble plus a tick reads as "message received" in every language, and the blue and green are the app's own information and yes colours.

Concept 2: the traveller raising a sign

Idea: the moment of use. A neutral signage figure, in the style of the toilet pictogram, holds a sign above their head, on taxi yellow.

Works because it shows the whole story in one glance (a person, a sign shown to someone) and shares its figure and flat style with the symbol library.

Concept 3: a luggage tag with a pin

Idea: the object a traveller already knows. A yellow luggage tag with a punched hole and string, carrying a blue map pin for "take me here", on green.

Works because a tag says "travel" and "this is mine, show it" at once, and the loop and hole give the silhouette a shape no other symbol in the set has.
