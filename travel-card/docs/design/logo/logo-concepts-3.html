App logo, round 3: the info card, refined

Round 2 variation 3, with a bigger card and no speech tail, in three versions. Draft, not validated. "Travel Card" is the working name; Askcard and Showmark stay as unchecked placeholder names, and no name may be assumed free (trademark, store and domain checks are still needed).

What changed. (1) The white card is bigger: 44 x 56 dp instead of 34 x 52 dp, about 35 % more area and 29 % wider, so it fills the safe zone (its rounded corners sit about 32 dp from the centre, inside the 33 dp safe radius, so a circle mask does not cut it). The blue card behind is enlarged the same way. (2) The speech tail is removed. Kept: yellow background, fanned blue card, orange header with three punched dots, large question mark. Badge trade-off (B and C): the real MOD-07 and MOD-03 artwork sits bottom left of the card, 27 dp wide, centred 20 dp from the middle. Its outer edge reaches about 34 dp, 1 dp beyond the 33 dp safe circle but inside the circle mask (about 36 dp), so a circle mask shows it whole but a very tight mask could touch it. To keep the badge clear of the question mark, the question mark moves 3.5 dp to the right in B and C. At 32 dp the badge is about 8 px, so its shape and colour read but the cutlery detail of C does not. The monochrome layers are single alpha shapes (no masks, so they convert to Android vector drawables).
