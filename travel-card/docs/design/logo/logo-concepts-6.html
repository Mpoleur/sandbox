App logo, round 6: L6 with an Earth and planes

Draft, not validated. A3 stays the approved fallback. The app is now called Travel Cards (name still needs trademark, store and domain checks; no check done here).

What L6 is. A3's white card, orange header, three dots and question mark, and the yellow background, with the blue card replaced by a round Earth: blue oceans, two simplified green continents (not a map, no borders or lines), and two small white planes with a trail of three white dots each. Trade-offs. To keep everything inside the 66 dp safe circle and still leave the Earth visible, the card is shown at 80 % of A3's size (32 x 50 dp instead of 40 x 62) and shifted up and right; its question mark gets a slightly heavier stroke and the gap between curve and dot is 4 dp (A3: 5), about 1.2 px at 32 dp. The Earth is mostly behind the card, so about half of it shows (left and bottom). The planes are about 13 dp and the trail dots 2 to 3 dp, so at 32 dp the planes are small marks and the dots almost vanish; at 48 dp they read. The monochrome layer is simplified: an arc of the Earth, one continent, one plane and the card outline (no trail dots, one continent and one plane dropped, so it stays readable at 32 dp). The plane is a generic top-view silhouette, drawn here, not an airline mark. Files: round6-L6-{icon,foreground,background,monochrome}.svg.
