App logo, round 4: A2, a taller card

Matthieu chose round 3 version A and asked for a taller card. Draft, not validated. "Travel Card" is the working name; Askcard and Showmark remain unchecked placeholder names (no trademark, store or domain check done).

A2 sizes. White card 40 x 62 dp (A: 44 x 56), corner radius 11 (A: 9), so the proportion goes from 1 : 1.27 to 1 : 1.55; 9 % narrower, 11 % taller, area about 1 % larger. Tilt stays -6 degrees. Because the safe zone is a circle, the tilt does not change how far the corners sit from the centre; what keeps the card inside is its half-diagonal (36.9 dp) minus the rounding of the corners (4.6 dp), which leaves the corners about 32.3 dp from the centre, inside the 33 dp safe radius. The blue card behind is 40 x 60 dp, tilted 19 degrees (A: 42 x 54, 16 degrees), tilted more so that it still shows beside the narrower white card. The header band is 15 dp as before with three punched dots; the question mark is re-centred in the taller white area under the header. Trade-off: the white card cannot be taller or wider than this without its corners leaving the safe circle (the blue card behind has its corners about 31.5 dp from the centre).
