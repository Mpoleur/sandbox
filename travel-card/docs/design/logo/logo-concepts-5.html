App logo, round 5: A3, a smaller question mark

Matthieu said A2 is almost right: the question mark is made smaller so the dot and the curve never touch. A3 approved by Matthieu as the app logo on 25/11/2026. "Travel Card" is the working name; Askcard and Showmark remain unchecked placeholder names (no trademark, store or domain check done).

A3 against A2. Only the question mark changed. Curve stroke 6 dp (A2: 7.5), curve width 22 dp outside to outside (A2: 27.5), dot radius 3.5 dp (A2: 4.3); the whole mark is 34 dp tall (A2: 38), so about 80 % as wide and 89 % as tall (about 82 % of the stroke and dot). The gap between the end of the stem (with its round cap) and the dot is 5 dp on the 108 dp canvas (A2: the two touched): about 4.4 px at 96 dp, 2.2 px at 48 dp and 1.5 px at 32 dp. The mark is re-centred under the header (its middle sits 0.5 dp from the middle of the white area). Everything else is exactly A2: white card 40 x 62 dp, radius 11, tilt -6 degrees, blue card 40 x 60 dp at 19 degrees, yellow background, orange header with three punched dots. The monochrome layer uses the same shapes, so it has the same gap.
