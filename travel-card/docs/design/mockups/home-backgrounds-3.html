Home D4 backgrounds: 3c, 3d, 3e and 4c

Draft for Matthieu, not validated. 3b and 4b are shown for reference. Only the screen background changes; everything else in D4 is unchanged, including the 88 dp bottom spacer. Flat vector only (paths, circles, dashes; no gradients, filters or bitmaps). The background is one fixed layer behind the scroll list, so it stays still while the cards scroll over it; tickets are opaque, so no shape is ever behind a ticket label. Contrast lines give ink against the ground and against the darkest tint a title could cross.
