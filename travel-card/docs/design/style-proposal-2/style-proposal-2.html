Travel Card artwork: style proposal 2 (sample, 13 symbols)

For Matthieu to confirm before any of the 168 are redrawn. Draft, not validated.

The idea. Recognition first: each object is a flat coloured silhouette in its real-world colour, with two tones of the same hue for detail and white highlights. No black outlines on food; dark ink is kept for people and neutral objects. Signage-style figures and badges (Toilet, Wi-Fi, modifiers) keep the spirit of the first draft, so the two families still sit together.
